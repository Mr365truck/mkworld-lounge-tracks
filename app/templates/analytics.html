How to read these numbers

SD — standard deviation of placements. Lower means more consistent; higher means more volatile.

Residual — placement minus the mean of the other races in that session. Negative is better than your session form.

95% CI — uncertainty range around the mean residual. A wide interval is uncertain; one spanning zero does not clearly show better or worse performance.

Weighted — residual × pick rate. Positive is expected places lost per session; negative is places gained, accounting for how often the track appears.

How to read the session model

Predictors — room max is the strongest rating, room avg is overall lobby strength, seat is your logged seat, and spread is max minus min MMR.

n is sessions with both values. r runs from −1 to +1 and gives direction and strength. Because a higher placement number is worse, positive r means the predictor rose as results worsened.

R² is the fraction of placement variation explained, from 0 to 1. Adjusted R² penalizes the four-predictor model for complexity.

p measures how surprising the relationship would be if the true linear relationship were zero. Smaller is stronger evidence; it does not measure effect size.

Multivariate fits all four predictors together. Its R² can look impressive with little data, so rely on the warning and adjusted R².

— means the number is not applicable or there is not enough varying data to estimate it.
